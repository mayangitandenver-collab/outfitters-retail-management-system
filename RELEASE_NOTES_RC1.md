# Outfitters Retail Management System — Release Candidate 1

ORMS RC1 consolidates the completed backend milestones:

- Multi-store foundation
- Authentication and role-based access control
- Catalog, variants, inventory, and transfers
- POS sales, returns, and cash sessions
- Purchasing and suppliers
- Reporting and analytics
- Customer loyalty and CRM
- Employee management, attendance, and audit logs
- Finance and accounting foundation
- Barcode aliases, notification queues, integrations, and ESC/POS receipts
- Production health endpoints and container deployment files

RC1 must complete user-acceptance testing before use with live financial data.
